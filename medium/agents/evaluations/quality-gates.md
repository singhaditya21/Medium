# Medium agent quality gates

Before a finding reaches the user, it must pass the applicable gates.

## Story brief

1. **Differentiation:** resolves a reader decision not already covered by the catalog.
2. **Evidence:** current/source-dependent claims use relevant primary sources and uncertainty is visible.
3. **Operating depth:** includes a realistic actor, workflow, control boundary, failure mode, and measurable outcome.
4. **Authenticity:** contains no invented first-person account, client, employer, quote, or result.
5. **Medium readiness:** identifies human-authorship/disclosure, paywall/distribution, canonical, rights, and image implications.

## Performance audit

1. **Metric integrity:** presentations, views, reads, read ratio, and traffic sources are not conflated.
2. **Sample discipline:** labels low-volume conclusions as hypotheses and waits for the appropriate checkpoint.
3. **Causal restraint:** distinguishes observed evidence from likely explanation and proposed test.
4. **Smallest intervention:** recommends the narrowest safe change with a measurable stop rule.

## Conversation candidate

1. **Relevance:** relates directly to an established content pillar and the source argument.
2. **Context:** source and existing responses were read enough to avoid repetition.
3. **Contribution:** adds an implementation implication, metric, evidence, or constructive challenge.
4. **Integrity:** avoids generic praise, self-promotion, engagement exchange, mass behavior, or unfounded claims.
5. **Approval readiness:** exact target, public link, exact text, score, and reason are visible.

Fail any gate: retain it only as a research note or discard it. Do not queue or act on it.
