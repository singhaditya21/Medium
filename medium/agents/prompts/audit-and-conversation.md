# Audit and conversation research prompt

For each Medium audit, report the evidence before the recommendation.

## Owned-story audit

1. Record story state, publication status, schedule, canonical URL, topics, subscriber-email intent, paywall state, featured image/preview, and internal-series links.
2. Capture the relevant presentation, view, read, read-ratio, follower/subscriber, and traffic-source evidence.
3. Classify the constraint: distribution, feed clickthrough, retention, audience conversion, accuracy, or insufficient data.
4. Recommend the smallest reversible intervention. Do not propose rewriting a long technical story on a tiny sample.
5. Give the metric, expected direction, checkpoint, and a stop rule.

## Scheduled-story audit

For every scheduled story, inspect and report all of the following before any other recommendation:

1. Exact title and scheduled publishing time in IST, including time remaining and whether it is overdue.
2. Current story state and publication destination.
3. Subscriber-email, paywall, public/unlisted, canonical, and topic settings.
4. Rendered preview title/subtitle and featured-image state.
5. AI disclosure, image-labeling, source, duplication, and release-readiness risks.
6. A clear no-change statement unless the user has approved an exact correction.

## Conversation research

1. Inspect up to five relevant public Medium stories, fully enough to understand their argument and existing thread.
2. Require a score of at least 0.70 from relevance, discussion quality, unique contribution, and recency.
3. Draft at most two responses that add a technical argument, implementation metric, evidence, or respectful challenge specific to the source.
4. Do not include self-promotional links, generic praise, bulk claps/highlights, or follow requests.
5. Present exact target, public link, evidence, exact response, score, and approval request in chat. Do not post it.
