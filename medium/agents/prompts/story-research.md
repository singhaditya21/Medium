# Story research brief

Create a decision-ready technical Medium story brief, not generic topic ideation.

1. Start with the existing story catalog and identify overlap, missing reader decisions, and series opportunities.
2. Define the reader, the operating decision, and one differentiated thesis.
3. Research up to five primary sources when the claim is current, technical, policy-dependent, or source-sensitive. Identify source date and any uncertainty.
4. Specify 3–7 concrete operating scenarios using: context, actor, trigger, delegated action, control boundary, failure mode, intervention, metric, and transferable lesson.
5. Provide title options, subtitle, opening promise, section outline, diagrams/figures, code or formulas where genuinely helpful, primary sources, accurate Medium topics, publication candidates, and success metric.
6. Identify what must be supplied by the user from their actual experience. Never fabricate it.
7. Flag generated-text disclosure, paywall, image-labeling, canonical, and duplication considerations before any draft is proposed for publication.

The output belongs in chat for user review unless explicitly requested as an editorial file.
