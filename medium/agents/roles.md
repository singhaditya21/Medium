# Medium role contracts

| Role | What it does | Outcome of a successful pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Catalog and gap analysis | Maps the existing catalog against content pillars, audience promise, and series coverage. | A prioritized gap or a reason not to create a duplicative story. |
| Scheduled-story and release audit | Inspects every scheduled story's timing and release configuration without changing it. | A release-risk alert or a verified-ready release record. |
| Source and story opportunity research | Finds differentiated technical questions and primary evidence. | A source-backed story brief with thesis, reader, decision, and counterargument. |
| Story architecture and visual planning | Plans outline, code, figures, artifacts, and series position before drafting. | A technically coherent build plan rather than decorative length. |
| Editorial, claims, and policy review | Checks claim support, originality, attribution, AI disclosure, image use, and distribution/paywall implications. | A publishable-risk assessment with exact changes, if any, for approval. |
| Performance and retention audit | Reads presentations, views, reads, read ratio, traffic, and conversion at due checkpoints. | A diagnosis that separates distribution from retention and insufficient sample size. |
| Read-through engineering | Tests the first-screen payoff, reader orientation, section pacing, figure usefulness, and internal navigation. | An evidence-led retention improvement packet only when a rewrite is warranted. |
| Distribution and publication fit | Evaluates topics, preview, canonical link, email intent, publication placement, and internal navigation. | An approval-ready distribution package or a reason to preserve current settings. |
| Conversation research and response drafting | Reviews a small number of relevant public stories for a substantive technical contribution. | At most two exact, source-specific response candidates for approval. |
| Growth experiment planning | Defines a measurable and reversible discovery, clickthrough, retention, or relationship test. | A hypothesis, metric, decision threshold, and review date. |
| Approval, receipts, and reporting | Presents exact public changes, obtains confirmation, verifies visible state, and records allowed evidence. | No public action without confirmation and a reliable audit trail after it. |

Shared agents provide the content graph, evidence supply chain, experiment discipline, technical-art direction, and portfolio-conversion analysis during these cycles. They do not create extra runs or alter Medium.
