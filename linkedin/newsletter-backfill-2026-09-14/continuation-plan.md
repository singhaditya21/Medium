## Editorial sequence and deliverables

1. **Execution correctness, M01–M05:** transaction boundaries, ambiguous outcomes, safe retries, independent verification and compiled policy. Each draft needs a concrete failure trace, typed contract or executable example, and explicit limits on the proposed design.
2. **Operations and ownership, M06–M10:** observability, degraded modes, fencing, incident accountability and change budgets. Include state transitions, recovery conditions, owner handoffs and a falsifiable operating metric.
3. **Data and contracts, M11–M15:** context boundaries, RevOps ledgers, versioned tools, shadow-mode data exposure and authorization-aware caches. Label illustrative numbers and distinguish proposed architecture from measured production evidence.
4. **Scale and business outcomes, M16–M20:** fair queues, entity merge recovery, irreversible email, contact policy and incremental value. Show denominators, assumptions, failure cases and measurement design; do not invent field experience.

Before approval, deliver for each edition: exact full-length body, fact/citation audit, technical diagrams and captions/alt text, title, cover, source disclosure, exact launch introduction, destination, audience, comments, subscriber-notification effect and proposed time. Preserve the original story's code, formulas and lists where supported; visibly inspect the native result. Animated launch media is a separate approval item where proposed, not a silent replacement for article figures.
