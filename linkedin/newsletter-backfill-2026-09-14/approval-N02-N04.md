## Exact N02 announcement

CRM can record every interaction and still leave the important work unfinished.

This edition examines the move from systems of record to bounded execution across five enterprise agent roles. Seven architecture figures connect orchestration, memory, tools, governance and human approval.

The practical question is not whether every workflow needs an agent. It is which decision benefits from reasoning, and which action should remain deterministic.

Where does your CRM still hand the hardest part back to a person?
