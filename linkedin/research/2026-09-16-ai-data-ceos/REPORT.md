## Quality and evidence rules

Comments use source-specific engineering implications, measurable operating outcomes and explicit questions. Proposed metrics are suggestions, not fabricated benchmark results. Source numbers are attributed; unverified competitive allegations are not endorsed. No claim is made to have watched unread videos, read linked reports, independently reproduced experiments, or inspected all comments on every source.

Only Barr Moses and Krishna Gade have final shortlist scores at this checkpoint. Other scores are intentionally unassigned rather than manufacturing discussion-quality evidence.
