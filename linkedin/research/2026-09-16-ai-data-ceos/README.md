## One-off scope

The user explicitly authorized 50 AI/data-platform CEOs and up to five recent,
relevant posts per CEO. This one-off 250-post research allowance does not change
the repository policy, recurring research limits, schedules, or action-time
approval requirement. No research comment is approved for publication.

“Top” means prioritized fit for enterprise AI, data platforms, agent controls,
CRM/RevOps, governance and measurable operating outcomes, not a claim of a
universal CEO popularity ranking. BUSINESSNEXT and EXL are excluded from proactive
outbound selection.

## Evidence standard

- Verify the current role using a current company source and/or the CEO's visible
  LinkedIn profile. Record the source and the verification date.
- Inspect LinkedIn through signed-in Chrome only. Do not use scraping, APIs,
  exported sessions, raw page dumps or browser-state artifacts.
- Select the latest relevant authored posts visible in the review, excluding
  empty reposts, generic congratulations, irrelevant hiring and sponsored posts.
- Capture public post URLs, displayed recency, a concise factual summary, the
  exact proposed response and review status. Do not manufacture five posts when
  fewer can be verified.
- Keep source claims distinct from proposed measures and illustrative examples.
- Recheck the full thread, prior activity, duplicate receipts, author identity,
  and freshness before proposing any publication batch. Research drafts with an
  incomplete thread review are explicitly marked provisional.
- Use the strategy score: 0.35 relevance + 0.25 discussion quality + 0.20 unique
  contribution + 0.20 recency. A score is editorial prioritization, not predicted
  reach or a benchmark. Require at least 0.70 for an execution proposal.
- One relevant author mention at most; no reach-only tags, hashtags, promotional
  links, automatic reactions, follows or DMs.
- Preserve watchlist waiting states. Five researched posts for one CEO are
  alternatives for selection, not a plan to comment on all five.
