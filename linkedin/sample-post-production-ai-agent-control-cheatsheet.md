## Post copy

An AI agent is not production-ready when the loop works.

It is production-ready when every material action crosses three proof boundaries:

1. **Decision proof** — fresh evidence, a typed plan and an explicit risk score.
2. **Authority proof** — a versioned policy decision, consequence-based approval and a short-lived action lease.
3. **Effect proof** — a fenced write, independent postcondition verification and a durable receipt.

The operating scorecard should track what average task accuracy hides:

- harmful-action rate;
- independent postcondition coverage;
- p95 authority-revocation time;
- duplicate-effect rate; and
- recovery success.

The animation shows the control hand-off from intent to verified outcome, including the branch that must stop, reconcile and recover when evidence is inconclusive.

Which boundary is most often missing in production: decision evidence, execution authority or outcome verification?
