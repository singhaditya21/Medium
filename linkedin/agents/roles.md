# LinkedIn role contracts

| Role | What it does | Outcome of a successful pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal triage | Sorts recent notifications, reactions, comments, mentions, and invitations by urgency and relationship significance. | A small, deduplicated inbound follow-up list. |
| Relationship context | Reconstructs approved history from the watchlist and receipts without retaining private message text. | Each follow-up has a real context and no premature repeat. |
| Relationship allocator | Allocates limited attention using relevance, relationship stage, reciprocity, freshness, and a contact-frequency cap. | The highest-value relationships receive the next appropriate action recommendation. |
| Network adjacency mapper | Maps nearby practitioner communities and credible paths between enterprise AI, CRM, RevOps, governance, and operations. | Relevant, non-random discovery targets and collaboration hypotheses. |
| Opportunity research | Reviews no more than five source-specific public profiles or posts per cycle. | Evidence-backed comment, reply, post, or relationship candidates. |
| Conversation-quality verifier | Tests novelty, factual grounding, relevance, and whether a draft advances the source discussion. | Low-value, generic, copied, or engagement-bait drafts are rejected. |
| Comment and reply drafting | Drafts concise source-specific comments or replies with an operating implication, metric, trade-off, or constructive challenge. | Exact approval-ready copy with target, evidence, tags, and timing. |
| DM drafting | Drafts a one-to-one note only where there is a genuine relationship reason. | A specific non-pitch outreach draft, or an explicit decision not to send one. |
| Post-format strategist | Selects native format, hook, proof point, asset, and timing hypothesis based on the post's goal. | A format brief that makes the eventual post easier to read and measure. |
| Post drafting | Creates an original post concept from the editorial catalog and performance evidence. | One approval-ready post draft, never an automatic publication. |
| Reputation-risk editor | Checks factual claims, tone, confidentiality, tagging, attribution, and potential misinterpretation. | A safer final draft or a clear stop/research recommendation. |
| Performance analysis | Compares post, profile, and relationship signals at their due checkpoints. | A metric-led decision to continue, test, revise, or stop. |
| Approval and receipts | Shows exact actions, captures user confirmation, verifies visible completion, and records privacy-safe evidence. | No external action without approval; verified actions have a credential-free receipt. |

All roles are research and drafting stages. They do not independently perform external actions.
