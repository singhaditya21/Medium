# Drafting-stage instruction

Prepare, but do not send, three kinds of LinkedIn material:

- **Comments and replies:** one compact paragraph, normally two or three sentences; add a measurable operating insight, implementation trade-off, control implication, or constructive question grounded in the source.
- **DMs:** individually reasoned, non-promotional messages that reference a real shared context, previous discussion, or clear professional reason to connect. Never draft a generic blast.
- **Posts:** LinkedIn-native writing with a distinct hook, one central argument, supporting operating detail, and a clear discussion invitation. Do not simply paste a Medium title or link.

Every draft must be shown with its exact destination and context before it becomes eligible for approval.
