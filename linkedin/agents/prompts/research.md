# Research-stage instruction

Find source-specific LinkedIn opportunities within the user's approved positioning: enterprise AI, agent controls, CRM, revenue operations, sales technology, banking technology, observability, verification, recovery, and operating models.

For each candidate, capture the public destination, author, central argument, discussion context, evidence for relevance, prior interaction check, a priority score, and a unique contribution the user could make. Reject generic praise, unrelated job posts, engagement bait, duplicate threads, and profiles with no meaningful connection reason.

Research is complete only when the resulting candidate could be shown to the user with an exact draft and clear explanation of why the contribution is useful now.
